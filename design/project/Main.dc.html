the radio

a ranch kid from Sunnyside, Washington, raised on bluegrass and nineties country radio

Zach Top grew up playing bluegrass in a family band before moving to Nashville. His songs lean on fiddle, steel guitar and a flat-top acoustic, the kind of traditional country you'd expect to hear coming out of a jukebox.

  • Sunnyside, Washington Raised on a ranch, and first on stage in Top String, the bluegrass band he started with his siblings.
  • Cold Beer & Country Music The 2024 debut country album, home of “Sounds Like the Radio” and “I Never Lie.”
  • Ain't in It for My Health The 2025 follow-up, with “Good Times & Tan Lines” and “South of Sanity.”
